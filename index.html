About

I automate the processes I used to run

I started at Nadir as a SENAI apprentice and spent the next years on the production floor as a mechanical fitter and machine setter: maintenance, failure analysis, operational reports and daily work in SAP.

That experience pushed me toward technology. In September 2025 I became a Systems Analyst, and now I build Power Apps, Power Automate flows and Power BI reports that replace manual work in operations, quality and management.

I'm finishing a Technologist Degree in Data Science with emphasis on AI, and I keep growing in SQL, Python, ETL and Microsoft Fabric.

What I care about: tools that operators actually use, data that leadership can trust, and automations that remove repetitive work instead of adding steps.

How I work

  1. Understand

    Start on the shop floor: watch the process, talk to the people who run it, find where time and errors go.

  2. Design

    Sketch screens and data flow with the users. Fewer fields, sensible defaults, clear states.

  3. Build

    Power Apps, Power BI or Python, whichever fits. Validations and error handling from day one.

  4. Automate

    Power Automate flows, scheduled refreshes and integrations remove the manual steps around the tool.

  5. Measure

    Structured data from day one, so the result shows up in a report and the next decision is easier.
